Demonstrators

A framework for developing demonstrators

Faizan Ahmed, Pieter Zeilstra, Sebastian Piest and Jeroen Linssen propose a framework for developing cyber-physical system demonstrators. Their 2026 paper uses a design science approach to connect stakeholder needs and objectives with the design and evaluation of a demonstrator.

The relevance cycle identifies the context, users, objectives and evaluation criteria. The design cycle develops and evaluates the physical and virtual parts of the demonstrator. The rigor cycle connects that work to existing knowledge and records what is learned.

Read the publication and access the paper · DOI: 10.5220/0014355700004055

How this demonstrator relates to the framework

The framework provides a way to understand this ZORRO demonstrator: the Fischertechnik factory is its physical system, while the knowledge graph, models and interactive tools are digital resources for exploring it. The project’s diagnostic goals and the needs of researchers and industrial partners provide the context for choosing what to demonstrate and evaluate.

The framework also highlights the role of documentation and feedback as the demonstrator develops. This website presents the factory components and available research tools as part of that ongoing work.
