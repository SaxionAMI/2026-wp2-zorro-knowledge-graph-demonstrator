WORK PACKAGE 2

Knowledge representation

WP2 makes knowledge about systems and failures explicit and reusable, helping people investigate faults in complex systems.

Explore the demonstrator contributions from Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam and the University of Twente below.

Demonstration by institution

VRIJE UNIVERSITEIT AMSTERDAM

VU research demonstration

Researcher Ameneh Naghdi Pour

With Benno Kruit, Jieying Chen, Stefan Schlobach and industrial collaborators Godfried Webers and Peter Kruizinga, this research investigates how to represent maintenance knowledge in an ontology and knowledge graph for fault diagnosis.

This site presents the VU work implemented for the demonstrator; additional project research and results are not yet showcased here. Explore the concepts, graph and queries that are available.

UNIVERSITY OF TWENTE

University of Twente research demonstration

Researchers Aimé Ntagengerwa and Erfan Nateghi

Aimé Ntagengerwa, Georgiana Caltais and Mariëlle Stoelinga investigated how to synthesise fault trees from knowledge graphs. The ZORRO demonstrator Capella model by Erfan Nateghi, Aimé Ntagengerwa and Georgiana Caltais provides another view of the factory system.

The model shown here is the UT work currently implemented for this demonstrator. The wider project includes additional research and results not yet showcased on this site.

Work package information

Leaders: Dr. Jieying Chen and Prof. Stefan Schlobach

Goal

Make knowledge explicit and formal so it can be reused. Formal knowledge models support more effective diagnosis and help reduce downtime in cyber-physical systems.
