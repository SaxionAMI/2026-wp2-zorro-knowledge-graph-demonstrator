University of Twente research demonstration

This demonstrator presents part of the University of Twente's WP2 work. The wider project includes additional research and results not yet shown on this site.

How the work relates to the factory

The Capella model offers a model-based view of the Fischertechnik factory. Its diagrams and documentation describe the system and provide another way to explore the demonstrator alongside the knowledge graph.

Research on synthesising fault trees from knowledge graphs investigates how structured knowledge can support reliability analysis. The paper describes this research; the site does not currently provide a fault-tree synthesis tool.
